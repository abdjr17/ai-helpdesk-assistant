# Connect to the company VPN from home
Keywords: vpn, remote, home office, homeoffice, work from home, secure connection, connect
Steps:
1. Check that your home internet works.
2. Open the VPN app and choose the company profile.
3. Sign in with your work account and approve the code on your phone.
4. Wait until the app shows "Connected".
5. Open the internal tool you need.
If this does not work: restart the VPN app, then restart the laptop. If it still fails, create a ticket with the exact error message.
