# Set up multi-factor authentication on a new phone
Keywords: mfa, authenticator, two-factor, 2fa, verification code, new phone, code, authentication
Steps:
1. Keep your old phone until the new one is working, if possible.
2. Install the approved authenticator app on the new phone.
3. On your computer, open the security settings of your account.
4. Add the new phone and scan the QR code.
5. Test with a sign-in and then remove the old phone.
If this does not work: create a ticket. IT will verify your identity before resetting your authentication.
