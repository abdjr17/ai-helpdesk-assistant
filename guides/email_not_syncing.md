# Email not syncing or not receiving new messages
Keywords: email, mail, outlook, inbox, sync, syncing, not receiving, messages, calendar
Steps:
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Check that Outlook is not in "Work offline" mode.
3. Close and reopen Outlook.
4. Check that your mailbox is not full.
5. Try the web version of your email to see if the messages are there.
If this does not work: create a ticket and say whether the web version works.
