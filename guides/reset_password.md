# Reset a forgotten password
Keywords: password, forgot, login, log in, sign in, locked, locked out, reset, account
Steps:
1. Go to the company sign-in page and click "Forgot password".
2. Enter your work email address and confirm with the code sent to your approved phone.
3. Choose a new password with at least 12 characters. Do not reuse an old password.
4. Sign in again on all your devices with the new password.
If this does not work: create a ticket and write your name and the time of the last successful login. Never send your password to anyone, including IT.
